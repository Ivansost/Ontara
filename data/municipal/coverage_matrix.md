Population ranking sums the 2021 Census counts of actual lower-tier members in Ontario’s current official registry. Separated single-tier cities and all nonmember census subdivisions are excluded; census-division totals are not substituted. Hamilton township is joined within Northumberland, avoiding confusion with the single-tier City of Hamilton. Halton was imported first for the demo despite rank 4. [Ontario registry](https://data.ontario.ca/en/dataset/municipalities), [Statistics Canada table 98-10-0004-01](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=9810000401).

Specific limitations include generally unverified sidewalk ownership; asset-specific curb responsibility; Renfrew’s linked form returning HTTP403; JavaScript-only portal questionnaires; and unverified urgency/tracking/repair targets where not published. Requirements and observed field labels are stored only when actually visible or explicitly stated. No resident data, report submission, phone call or live email was used for this research.
